About

Space Type is an innovative typing game that combines fast-paced action with immersive space exploration. Players will pilot their spaceship through various cosmic environments, battling alien threats and navigating asteroid fields, all while improving their typing skills.

Features

Difficulty & Speed Scaling

The game features a progressive difficulty curve. As your score increases, the velocity of falling asteroids accelerates.

Data-Based Word Categories

The game utilizes a data-driven approach to manage word categories, allowing for easy updates and expansions.

Character Selection

Before the game begins, players can choose through different astronaut profiles (Ranger, Blaze, Volt, Luna, Ghost).
