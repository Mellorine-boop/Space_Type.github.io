ABOUT SPACE TYPE

Learn more about the game and its development.

Game Overview

Space Type is a fast-paced typing defense game where players must destroy falling words before they reach the ground. Each word represents an incoming threat, and the player must quickly type the correct word to eliminate it and earn points. The game features multiple difficulty levels, different word categories, and special word types that introduce unique effects such as bonus lives, score boosts, and temporary slowdowns. As the player progresses, the speed and intensity of the game increase, testing both accuracy and reaction time. With its simple controls but progressively challenging gameplay, Space Type combines typing skills with real-time decision making in a space-themed survival setting.

Story

In the year 2087, humanity has expanded its reach into the cosmos. Space Type follows the journey of a young pilot navigating deep space, where asteroid rains constantly fall from unknown regions of the galaxy. These asteroid storms are not random—they are coded threats that must be neutralized before they reach the ship. To survive, the pilot must quickly decode and type the correct words attached to each incoming asteroid. Every successful input destroys an asteroid before impact, while missed ones bring the ship closer to destruction.

Our Vision

Our Vision Our vision is to create an engaging and educational typing game that immerses players in a thrilling space adventure while helping them improve their typing skills.
